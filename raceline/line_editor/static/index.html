Map

Drag a point to move the line: neighbours follow with a brush falloff. Drag empty space or right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Points you dragged form the edited region, tinted yellow; Smooth edited region rounds it off without moving anything else. Red rings mark body margin under the safety value. Dashed grey is the line as loaded.

Speed

Everything drawn is a layer on top of the physics profile (dashed when edits exist; the file's own column in grey). Draw sets the curve under the mouse and smooths the stroke on release. Smooth and Erase act under the brush. Zone drags a level to add a speed or lateral zone, which the profile solver honours. Lap time and the implied acceleration update live while you draw; the server confirms on release. The lap is a closed loop: the shaded ↺ margins at both ends of the axis show the loop continuing, and a stroke can be drawn across the start line. The minimap in the sidebar shows where the cursor is on the track; hover or click it to jump.
