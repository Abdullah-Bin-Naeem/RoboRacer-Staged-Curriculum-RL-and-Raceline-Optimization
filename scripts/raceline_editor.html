How

Map — drag a handle to move the line. Double-click the line to add a handle, double-click a handle to remove it.

Profile — drag to pin a speed. A pin is a cap: the passes brake into it and accelerate out of it. Pinning above the grip ceiling does nothing, and shows greyed. Double-click a pin to clear it.

Widths, curvature and the profile are re-derived on every edit; the saved file is always uniformly re-sampled and closed.
